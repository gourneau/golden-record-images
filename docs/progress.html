Settled, and it ends the searchthe ceiling

The most consequential measurement here. The recording chain could have carried more than 1000 brightness elements per stripe — its own edge response is 1.75–2.37 samples. The 1977 television camera could not.

We already sample above the camera’s Nyquist in both axes. Every element it captured is in these pictures, and anything sharper would be invented. Independently on the calibration ring: MTF50 0.102 cycles/px, line-spread 5.1 px, ~77 resolvable elements per trace.

What cheating would buythe oracle tier

A deliberately dishonest decoder, allowed to pick its settings per image by looking at the true slide. Useless as a decoder — it needs the answer to produce the answer — but the gap measures what outside knowledge is worth.

A metric that liesworth knowing

The internal quality composite rewards blur. On the calibration frame: clean 88.5, Gaussian σ=1 → 99.0, σ=2 → 100.0. It scored a term measuring the picture’s diagonality rather than the decoder’s health, so it anti-correlated with sharpness (−0.169). Fixing that cut the reward for blur from +28 to +7 but did not remove it. It is a timing-damage detector, never a quality score.

Two metrics that do behave: MTF50 from the ring falls monotonically under blur (0.102 → 0.087), and gradient correlation against the real slide falls hard (0.470 → 0.304). Those are the arbiters.

Retractedkept on the record

Three findings were published here and later withdrawn. They are listed because the corrections are more instructive than the claims were.

ClaimedActually
The parity alternation is 60 Hz mains hum at ~70% of picture amplitude Measured over the whole record it sits at 60.0436 Hz — half the scan rate, not mains. Over one 512-trace frame those hypotheses are 0.21 FFT bins apart, so the original test could never have separated them. The 70% was measured inside the sync burst, leaking through a coordinate bug; in the picture it is 5–30%.
512 traces, 4:3 and the dot count are mutually inconsistent A bounding-box measurement of a thin ring in a resampled image. A proper ellipse fit on the native grid disagrees. The contradiction was ours.
A constant black block precedes every frame It is the diagonal hatch marker — picture-band std 0.055–0.101 against 0.005 for real gap. That premise was passed into a task and was wrong.
The decoder never reads a reference image. decode.py imports only numpy, dotclock and sync; its sole data input is the WAV. Original photographs are used for verification and labelling — confirming the frame-to-image mapping, confirming rotations, choosing the colour separation order — never for pixel recovery. An attempt to fit the droop against reference images failed and was not shipped.
